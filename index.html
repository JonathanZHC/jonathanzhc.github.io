PhD student · Robotics · Safe control

Haocheng Zhao

I study how robots can act safely in cluttered, dynamic and partially known environments, with safety guarantees that hold in real time on real hardware.

I am a PhD student at the Learning Systems and Robotics Lab at the Technical University of Munich, advised by Prof. Angela P. Schoellig. My work centres on safe human-robot interaction: safety filters built on control barrier functions, the geometric representations that make them reliable, and learning-based model predictive control for agile systems. Before the PhD I completed my Master's in Mechatronics and Robotics at TUM.

Research focus

Safety filters that stay reliable

Control-barrier-function filters that minimally modify a commanded motion, with barriers chosen so that the underlying optimisation remains well-conditioned and feasible in practice.

Geometry for real-time collision avoidance

Compact shape representations such as superquadrics, signed distances computed with GJK/EPA, and closed-form gradients that make whole-body collision avoidance fast enough for 100 Hz control.

Learning-based control for agile systems

Model predictive control that improves from experience, demonstrated on quadrotor racing, and policies for contact-rich manipulation deployed on real robots.

    Haocheng Zhao*, Lukas Brunke*, Oliver Lagerquist, Siqi Zhou, Angela P. Schoellig (*equal contribution)
