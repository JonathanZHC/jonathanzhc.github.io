About

I study how robots can act safely in cluttered, dynamic and partially known environments.

I am a PhD student at the Learning Systems and Robotics Lab at TUM, advised by Prof. Angela P. Schoellig. My research is on safe human-robot interaction, in particular safety filters built on control barrier functions and the geometric representations that make them reliable and fast enough for real-time use.

Before that, I completed my Master's in Mechatronics and Robotics at TUM, where I worked on learning-based model predictive control for agile quadrotor flight.

  • Safety filters & control barrier functions
  • Collision avoidance
  • Learning-based MPC
  • Robot manipulation
