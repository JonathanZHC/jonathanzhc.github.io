About

I am a PhD student at the Learning Systems and Robotics Lab at the Technical University of Munich, working with Prof. Angela P. Schoellig. My research focuses on safe human-robot interaction: how a robot can act in cluttered, dynamic and partially known environments while keeping formal safety guarantees.

I am particularly interested in safety filters built on control barrier functions, geometric representations that make such filters reliable and fast enough for real-time use, and learning-based control for agile systems. I received my M.Sc. in Mechatronics and Robotics from TUM.
